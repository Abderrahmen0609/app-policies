BarberShop Privacy Policy

WeCareAboutYou · Updated 14 October 2026

Scope and publisher

BarberShop is published by WeCareAboutYou. This notice describes the 1.5.3 offline guest testing preview, not a connected future marketplace. Contact contact@mellitinexus.com for privacy or testing help.

No connected app collection

This build does not connect to a backend, create online accounts, upload photos, request device location, contact other people, publish posts, take payment or externally share business content. The verified preview profile removes app network and location permissions. Bundled people, bookings, shop figures, products and articles are samples.

Local sample entries

Text you enter changes the in-memory sample scenario only. Do not enter real personal or sensitive information. Reset sample data or close/restart to discard changes. Report/block actions in this preview change only the sample scenario and do not send a report to staff.

Public policy browser handoff

The notice is readable offline. If you explicitly choose Read full privacy policy, the app opens the hosted policy in your browser. The browser/hosting service handles that page request under its own practices. This is not a backend connection or an account in the offline preview. Android and Google Play manage installation, updates and platform diagnostics under their settings/policies.

Deletion and audience

There is no online app account to delete in this preview. Reset/restart removes the in-memory sample changes; uninstall/clear-storage behavior is governed by Android. This testing preview is intended for adults. Bundled hair/scalp information is general education, not medical advice. No advertising, billing or live business-operation service is activated.
