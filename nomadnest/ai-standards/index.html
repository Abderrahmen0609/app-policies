NomadNest AI Content Standards

WeCareAboutYou · Updated 14 October 2026

Room planning, not a guarantee

AI generates optional room-layout/image ideas. Outputs may be inaccurate or unsuitable. Check furniture sizes, doors, structural limits, fire access, mobility needs and walking clearances yourself. The feature does not produce a measured room scan, professional building advice, a checkout or a full edited video.

Permitted content

Use room images and instructions you have permission to process. Do not request illegal, exploitative, hateful, harassing, sexually explicit or deceptive content, or submit another person’s private information unnecessarily. This adult testing release is intended for home-planning use.

In-app reporting

Use Report AI concept or Report layout suggestions on the displayed result. Report a layout issue also supports a planning result without a cloud request reference. A verified sign-in is required to submit the bounded report; it is separate from AI generation and does not spend the AI generation allowance. Only the reference/type, selected category and note you write go to a private support queue. Room images/videos, generated images, layouts and prompts are not automatically attached.

Response and choices

Submission confirms receipt only. The publisher can review reports and take appropriate action or improve the service; automatic detection, an immediate response or a guaranteed outcome is not promised. Contact contact@mellitinexus.com if needed. You can delete local room data, clear app-specific AI history or request account deletion using the privacy/deletion resources.
