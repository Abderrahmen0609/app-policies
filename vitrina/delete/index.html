Delete your Vitrina account

WeCareAboutYou · Updated 14 October 2026

Request deletion of your Vitrina account

Email contact@mellitinexus.com from the address registered to your Vitrina account, with subject “Vitrina account deletion”. The mailbox is monitored by the publisher. The same request path is linked prominently in Settings, including from the signed-out support screen. Do not send your password, sign-in codes or payment credentials.

What happens next

The publisher checks that the request belongs to the account holder, may ask for clarification of the marketplace account/content to remove, then explains any applicable retention and sends a completion notice. No fixed completion period is promised. If you no longer control your registered email, contact the same mailbox so the publisher can discuss another verification method.

Data covered by the request

The request covers the Supabase sign-in account and its associated Vitrina profile, favorites, owned listings/shops/worker profiles, reviews, conversations/messages and uploaded media, plus owned rules/block/report receipts. Media and business content require explicit operator cleanup; deleting only a public profile row or signing out is not a complete deletion. Ownership of older shops will be verified rather than guessed from a matching display name.

Delete selected data without closing your account

You can also request removal of specific Vitrina data while keeping your sign-in account. Email contact@mellitinexus.com from your registered address with subject “Vitrina data deletion”, and identify the owned listings, photos, shop/worker profile information or other account records you want removed. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes. The publisher verifies ownership and scope, removes the requested data and associated media as appropriate, and explains any limited retention or effects on shared records before confirming completion. You can delete your own listings or owned shops using the app; associated uploaded media can require this verified request. This data-only request does not close your sign-in account.

Possible retained copies

The publisher explains any limited data retained for legal obligations, unresolved safety issues or another person’s independent records. Shared content may already have been copied by recipients or processed in service-provider/platform logs. Where data cannot be erased immediately, the publisher explains the scope and reason. Local device erasure is a separate control.
