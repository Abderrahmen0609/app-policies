NexusConnect Privacy Policy

WeCareAboutYou · Updated 14 October 2026

Scope and publisher

NexusConnect is published by WeCareAboutYou. This notice covers the local card/contact workspace and optional hosted professional network in the 1.5.2 testing candidate. Contact contact@mellitinexus.com for privacy, deletion or safety requests.

Local workspace

Card profile/designs, selected logo/background copies, contacts and notes, follow-up dates, appearance choices, event plans and imported ticket/CV copies are stored in device app storage. They are not automatically synchronized. The app adds no separate encryption layer to these local records. Keep your device protected. Clearing app storage removes local records, subject to platform behavior.

Optional professional network

Email-code/link verification or an existing account password authenticates a Melliti Nexus account through Supabase over HTTPS. App session tokens stay in memory. The service stores account email/identifier, explicitly saved public profile and private contact fields, invitations, separately approved chat messages, blocks, entitlements and abuse counters. Password inputs are processed by Auth and are not placed in local card records. Premium testing is server-granted; this candidate does not collect payment credentials.

Visibility and permissions

Directory appearance is opt-in. Public names, self-described professions, city and short bio can be searched by entitled members. Contact details require mutual approval, and chat requires separate approval. Blocking/revocation denies future access; it cannot retract information someone already copied. Profession descriptions are user supplied and are not verified professional qualifications.

OCR and SDK metrics

Printed-card recognition processes the selected photo and recognized text on the device using Google ML Kit. The photo is not attached to the saved contact or uploaded by OCR. Users must check recognized fields. ML Kit may send installation identifiers, device/app details and performance/usage events to Google for diagnostics and analytics; this differs from uploading card image/text content. Google documents this processing at https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

Publishing rules and safety reports

Connected publishing and messaging require acceptance of the current community standards. Report controls send only the selected member/message reference, category and note you explicitly write to a private support queue. They do not automatically attach a contact/card/CV/photo/message snapshot. Reports are readable only by authorized service operators. The app does not promise automatic detection, immediate moderation or a fixed response time. You can separately block, revoke or opt out.

Retention and deletion

Local records stay until you remove them or clear app storage. Hosted network records stay while used unless removed through app-specific data clearing or a verified deletion request. The current app-specific network deletion keeps the shared sign-in identity, its community-rule acceptance record and other apps’ data. Safety reports are handled separately; the service runs daily report maintenance to remove reports after 90 days, which may add up to a day between scheduled runs. The acceptance record remains while the shared Auth identity remains. Full shared-account removal cascades owned reports and acceptance; app-only clearing is separate. Auth/provider logs and operational backups can follow separate provider retention. Request account deletion using the page linked below; identity and scope are verified before action, and completion is confirmed without a fixed-time promise.

Sharing and external resources

A vCard, QR, printed/exported PDF or shared CV includes the fields/file you choose. Recipients can retain them. File/photo selection reads the selected file, not a synchronized photo library. Clipboard, sharing, printing, policy pages and external profile links hand content/requests to your chosen destination, which has separate privacy practices. Supabase processes hosted data; Google processes the SDK metrics described above.

Audience and choices

This professional testing release is intended for adults. It is not intended for children. Contact the publisher if personal information was entered in error, if you need to correct it or if you want to discuss deletion. No advertising SDK or paid subscription checkout is included in this candidate.
