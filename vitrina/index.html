Vitrina Privacy Policy

WeCareAboutYou · Updated 14 October 2026

Who operates Vitrina

Vitrina is published by WeCareAboutYou. Contact contact@mellitinexus.com. This notice describes the connected marketplace in the new Android package com.mellitinexus.vitrina, version 1.1.0. The older vitrina.app testing application is a separate installation and can have different controls. Vitrina is intended for adults 18 and older.

Account and marketplace data

Supabase provides email/password authentication and stores the marketplace database and chosen uploads. Data can include your user identifier, name, email, phone, profile details, listing titles/descriptions/prices/categories/address, photos, shop/worker contact and service details, reviews, favorites, conversation membership and messages. Choose only details and photos you have permission to publish. Vitrina does not process in-app payments or guarantee an agreement between members.

Public content and private records

Other signed-in members can see published listings, shop/worker information and reviews, subject to the app’s blocking controls. Public image links can be opened or shared outside the app; blocking does not revoke those links. Shop or worker contact details you deliberately publish can be visible to others. Your sign-in email and private account profile are restricted to your own account in the app; a shop email you choose to publish is public. Conversations are restricted to their participants. Recipients may keep copies or contact you through contact details you share.

Optional device location and photos

You can select photos to upload. Uploaded files use your account’s storage path. Where device location is available and you grant permission, Vitrina can use it to suggest a service/listing area. Only approximate area coordinates are sent to the marketplace: the app reduces their precision into neighbourhood cells at least about five kilometres across. The new Android build requests coarse location only. You can decline device location and type an address or service area; do not publish your exact home address. The app does not request background location.

Rules, reporting and blocking

Before publishing or messaging, verified members accept the marketplace rules. The server stores the rules version and acceptance time. Reports contain a content reference, category, your note, reporter identifier and review status/time; no conversation or photo snapshot is automatically copied into a report. Reports are kept in a private publisher review queue. Blocks are stored as the two member identifiers and time; they prevent subsequent in-app messages in both directions and hide that member’s offers. Blocking does not prevent contact outside the app.

Device storage and providers

The app can retain its sign-in session and appearance preferences on the device. Clear this device’s data in Settings signs you out locally and removes local preferences; it does not remove server content or your account. New Android builds exclude local data from Android backup and device transfer. Inter fonts are bundled in the app rather than fetched from Google Fonts. No third-party advertising or analytics SDK or in-app payment SDK is used by the inspected source; featured/promoted marketplace content can still appear. Supabase and distribution/platform services may process request metadata and operational logs under their own practices. This notice does not promise zero provider logging or retention.

Retention and account deletion

Account and marketplace data can remain while you use the service until removed through supported app controls or a verified account-deletion request. Reports and rules/block receipts are handled separately from local sign-out. The publisher can review reports and use service-only maintenance; no automatic daily schedule or fixed report-retention period is promised here. Request account deletion through Settings or the web page below, or email contact@mellitinexus.com from your registered address. We verify identity and scope, remove associated account/content/media as appropriate, and explain any limited retention needed for legal or safety reasons and provide a completion notice. We do not promise a universal fixed completion time. Copies retained independently by recipients or service providers may be outside the publisher’s direct control.

Your choices and support

Use Settings for privacy, marketplace rules, blocked-member management, support, account-deletion requests and clearing this device’s data. You can delete your own listings or owned shops; deleting a listing does not itself establish removal of every previously uploaded image. Use the deletion request for complete associated-media removal. Do not email your password or sign-in codes. Contact contact@mellitinexus.com for questions about these data practices.
