CleanRoutine Privacy Policy

Updated 16 October 2026. Publisher: WeCareAboutYou. Operator: Melliti Nexus.

Version scope: This policy describes version 1.1.0 with optional household sharing. Earlier 1.0.2 testing previews provide device-local example records and completion changes; they do not offer accounts or shared households. Feature availability depends on your installed version.

Your local home

CleanRoutine lets you create rooms, cleaning tasks, due dates, recurrence, local member names and notes, and record or undo completion with contribution totals. This information stays in app preferences when you use the local home. Preferences have no additional app-level encryption. Older saved preference records are retained during migration, so clearing only a visible task does not erase all historical preference originals. Android app backup is disabled.

Optional account and household sharing

Sharing uses Supabase over HTTPS. Requesting an email code sends your email address to the Auth provider; entering the code creates or confirms a Melliti Nexus sign-in identity. An existing confirmed account with a password may instead use ordinary password sign-in. This option does not create or reset a password. Passwords are used by the Auth provider, are not stored in household records, and are not sent to other household members.

The same Auth identity can be used by other connected Melliti Nexus apps. CleanRoutine household records use their own membership-checked API and private database schema. Signing in, listing or selecting households does not upload your original local home. The Auth provider also processes authentication events, timestamps, account identifiers, IP addresses and user-agent information for sign-in, session operation and security. Native sessions use protected device storage; the browser preview keeps sessions in memory.

What household members see

A shared household contains its name, room names and types, task names, notes, schedules, priorities, estimated minutes, member display names and completion history. These records are available to authorized household members. Shared completion records identify the signed-in completing member. Owners can invite or remove members, rename or delete their household. Members can edit rooms and tasks and record completion; undo is limited to the completing member or household owner.

Creating a household offers a separate, unchecked-by-default option to import local rooms, tasks and completion history. The confirmation explains that task notes and local contributor names are included. Local member email addresses, device-only home metadata and undo snapshots are omitted. Original local records remain separate on your device. Import only information you are comfortable sharing with the household.

Invitations and security

Invitations work once and expire after 72 hours. Share invitation codes privately. The server stores a digest instead of the original code. Invitation issuance and joining are rate limited; membership and owner permissions are checked on the server. Shared edits require an internet connection. Version conflicts ask you to refresh rather than silently overwrite another member's changes. These controls do not prevent an authorized member from keeping a copy of information they have seen.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you change or delete it. To erase all current local records, legacy preference originals and the device's saved sign-in, use Android Settings > Apps > CleanRoutine > Storage > Clear data. This does not delete cloud households or information other people copied.

In Household sharing, an owner can delete a shared household and its records for every member. A member can leave to end their own access. Delete my CleanRoutine cloud data deletes your owned households, removes your memberships and CleanRoutine invitation/rate-limit links, and anonymizes your completion identity in other owners' households. Account-linked completion identifiers are anonymized. Room or task text you added, and local contributor names imported into a household owned by someone else, can remain in that household. Ask its owner or support for content-specific removal if that text identifies you. Records entered by other members and copies outside the app may also remain. These app-only controls do not delete your shared Melliti Nexus Auth identity or other apps' records.

For deletion of the shared Auth identity or a support-assisted request, use the deletion request page or email support. We verify ownership and explain the effect on other connected apps before processing shared-identity deletion. A support request may itself contain personal information you choose to provide; include only what is needed. Provider service/security records and legally required records may have separate retention.

Expired, revoked or consumed invitation codes stop working immediately. A daily server cleanup is configured to remove invitation metadata seven days after expiry and join-attempt metadata older than 24 hours. Removal occurs when that cleanup runs; service interruptions can delay it. Leaving a household, revoking an invitation or deleting your app data applies the access changes described above without waiting for the scheduled cleanup.

Other services and audience

The app bundles its fonts and images. Version 1.1.0 has no advertising or analytics SDK, AI calls, purchases, billing, automatic household rotation or push reminders. Opening an external policy page or email app is your choice; those services handle connection and message information under their own policies. The closed test is intended for adults.

Contact

For privacy, deletion and support, email contact@mellitinexus.com. Do not send passwords, email codes or sensitive household notes.
