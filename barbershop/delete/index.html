Request BarberShop account deletion

Barbershop Connect · Publisher: WeCareAboutYou · Operator: Melliti Nexus
Prepared 17 October 2026 · contact@mellitinexus.com

Request deletion without installing the app

  1. Email contact@mellitinexus.com from the email address used for your BarberShop account. Use the subject BarberShop account deletion.
  2. Say that you want your BarberShop account and associated app data deleted. If you want a narrower request, explain the data concerned. Do not include a password, email code, identity document or payment information.
  3. We will verify account ownership and the request scope before taking action, and tell you when the request is completed or explain any unresolved cleanup or retained records. We do not promise a fixed completion time.

This request concerns BarberShop. It does not request deletion of unrelated apps, email-provider accounts or records held independently by a business or another participant.

Deletion inside the connected app

Open the connected app's account settings and choose account deletion. Re-enter your password and type DELETE. If you already enrolled a verification factor, complete its challenge when asked. A signed-out user can also use the account-deletion route in the testing web client when that client is available; the email request above remains available without it.

If you cannot sign in or the service is unavailable, use the email request. Do not repeatedly create a new account to request deletion.

What deletion covers and what can remain

The connected deletion flow removes your authentication account and account-owned application records through the database relationships. It queues owned public/private photos for cleanup across services. Cleanup can remain pending during an outage or where a permitted existing reference prevents immediate removal. The operator must check pending cleanup rather than imply all media has already disappeared.

Restricted safety reports, reported snapshots, moderation decisions, appeal/suspension history and stable safety identifiers currently have no complete automatic expiry/deletion lifecycle in the testing implementation and can outlive account deletion. Support emails are handled separately. Other people may have copied public material, saved an externally shared poster/photo or kept a message independently. We will explain the scope of your request and any remaining records; no blanket immediate-erasure guarantee is made.

Leaving a shop, blocking an account, cancelling an appointment, uninstalling or clearing device storage is different from deleting your connected account. In the 1.5.3 offline guest preview there is no online account: reset or restart discards in-memory sample changes.
