NexusConnect Account Deletion

WeCareAboutYou · Updated 14 October 2026

Request deletion

Email contact@mellitinexus.com with the subject NexusConnect account deletion request. Include your app name and account email, and say whether you want this app’s data removed or the whole shared Melliti Nexus account removed. Do not send a password, verification code, government ID, payment details or unnecessary private content.

Identity and scope

The publisher verifies that the requester controls the account before deleting data, and may ask you to reply from the account email. A shared sign-in account can be used by other suite apps. Deleting it can affect those apps; the scope will be confirmed first. Sending this request does not itself immediately delete an account.

What is removed or retained

A completed request covers the account and associated app data within the verified scope. The publisher will explain any information that must be retained for security or legal reasons and confirm completion. Provider operational logs/backups may follow separate retention. No fixed completion time is promised. The device’s local copies and information already exported to recipients must be handled separately.

Separate app controls

In Professional network, Delete network account clears NexusConnect network records while retaining the shared sign-in identity and local workspace. Use this web request for full account deletion. Local contact/event deletion and app storage controls remain separate.
