CoParent community rules

Publisher: WeCareAboutYou · Updated 14 October 2026

Keep this adult community useful, respectful and safe. Accept these rules before publishing content.

Allowed use

Share general experiences, practical solutions and relevant articles. Speak respectfully and describe situations without identifying children or private family members. This is peer discussion, not a medical, legal or emergency service.

Prohibited content

Do not post threats, harassment, hateful or abusive content, sexual content involving minors, child exploitation material, violence encouragement, self-harm encouragement, fraud, unlawful activity, spam or unauthorized advertising. Do not publish someone else’s identifying, contact, location, medical or confidential information. Do not impersonate another person or claim professional authority you do not have.

Protect children and private information

Remove children’s names, school/location, photos, dates combined with ages, medical identifiers and contact details. An automatic filter cannot detect every identifying detail. You are responsible for the information you choose to publish.

Reports and blocks

Use Report on a post to send a reason to the publisher and hide it from your view. Use Block to limit mutual post visibility. Contact contact@mellitinexus.com for additional safety concerns. Controls and operator review do not guarantee a response time or continuous moderation.

Content controls

Authors can edit or remove their posts. The publisher can review reports and apply content/access controls available in the service. Follow the privacy policy and do not use the community as an emergency channel.
