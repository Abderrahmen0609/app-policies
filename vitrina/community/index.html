Vitrina Marketplace Rules

WeCareAboutYou · Updated 14 October 2026

Adult marketplace and accurate offers

Vitrina is for adults 18 and older. Publish accurate descriptions and only items, services and photos you have the right to offer. Do not publish another person’s private information without permission.

Prohibited content

Illegal or dangerous goods, scams, impersonation, harassment, threats, hate, sexual content, exploitation and child sexual abuse or exploitation are prohibited. Do not use listing, shop, worker, review or chat features to post such content.

Trading and privacy

Agree payment and delivery directly. Vitrina does not process payments or guarantee transactions. Use a public meeting area; avoid publishing your exact home address. Marketplace content and public media links can be copied or shared outside Vitrina.

Report and block

Use the content-safety menu on listings, shops and worker profiles, or Report message in a conversation, to submit a private content reference and your note to the publisher’s queue. You can block members and manage your blocks in Settings. Blocking prevents subsequent in-app messages in both directions and hides that member’s offers, but cannot prevent contact outside the app. For immediate danger, contact the appropriate local authorities; Vitrina is not an emergency service.

Publisher contact

Contact contact@mellitinexus.com for support or account deletion. Accepting these rules does not authorize sharing another person’s private data. The server stores the rules version 2026-10-14 and the acceptance time for verified members.
