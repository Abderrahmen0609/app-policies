NexusConnect Community Standards

WeCareAboutYou · Updated 14 October 2026

Adult professional testing

Use this connected testing service only if you are 18 or older. Publishing a profile or sending a message requires explicit acceptance of rules version 2026-10-14. This is a conduct requirement, not proof of a person’s age, profession or identity.

Respect and authenticity

Use truthful professional descriptions. Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, scam or spam anyone. Do not post hate, sexual exploitation, illegal material or content that violates another person’s rights. Do not publish or share someone else’s private contact or personal information without permission. Do not attempt to evade a block, revoked connection or a declined invitation.

Permission before contact

Directory appearance is optional. Private contact exchange requires both people’s consent. Chat requires separate approval. Approval is not permission for harassment or unrelated marketing. A profession entered in a profile is self-described and not a verified license or qualification.

Report and protect

Use Report person/contact or Report this message to send a reason and optional note to the private support queue. No automatic contact or message snapshot is attached. Blocking, revoking chat/connection and hiding your profile are separate controls available without renewed publishing consent. You can also contact contact@mellitinexus.com.

Handling reports

The publisher can review the bounded report and relevant available service records, contact the participants as appropriate and take proportionate action. No automated detector, guaranteed decision or fixed response time is promised. Report receipt confirms submission only. Do not submit false reports or unnecessary sensitive data. Private reports are maintained separately from publicly visible content.
