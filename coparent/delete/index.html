CoParent account and data deletion

Publisher: WeCareAboutYou · Updated 14 October 2026

The local workspace, CoParent shared data and the suite-wide sign-in identity have different deletion scopes.

Request account deletion

Email contact@mellitinexus.com from the address associated with your sign-in. Use the subject CoParent account deletion request. State whether you want CoParent data/account access removed or the shared suite sign-in identity removed as well. Do not send your password, one-time code, child records or medical documents.

The publisher needs to verify ownership and the requested scope before account removal. After ownership and scope are verified, the publisher coordinates removal and confirms the result. This page opens an email request; it does not itself authenticate a user or execute deletion.

Remove CoParent shared data in the app

In Shared messages or Community, open Shared account → Delete CoParent shared data. The confirmation explains that shared families and conversations involving you, your CoParent profile/posts/reports/blocks and related rate records are removed. Other parents can lose access to those shared conversations. The shared email identity and other apps’ data are kept by this action.

Remove local information

Use local-data controls where available, or Android Settings → Apps → CoParent → Storage → Clear data. Local records and attachments are separate from connected-service records. Exported files and existing external backups must be removed separately.

Shared identity and provider copies

The sign-in identity is used across connected suite apps. Do not request identity deletion unless you also intend its effect on those apps. Provider security logs and backups may be subject to separate policies; no verified universal immediate-erasure promise is made.
