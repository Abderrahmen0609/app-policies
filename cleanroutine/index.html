CleanRoutine Privacy Policy

Updated14 October2026. Applies to testing version1.0.2.

Scope

This policy covers the current CleanRoutine closed-testing preview. The app provides sample household room and task lists and stores local completion changes. Accounts, household synchronization, AI plans, notifications and payments are not implemented.

Information on your device

Room and task records, completion status, and bundled household/member/contribution examples are stored in local app preferences. This storage has no additional app-level encryption. Sample contribution charts do not represent a real household.

Network services

The app bundles its fonts and sample assets. It does not download fonts at runtime and does not send local task or document records to an account, synchronization or AI server. This version contains no advertising or analytics SDK. Opening the online privacy policy or an email app is a user-initiated external action; the destination service handles connection or message information under its own policies.

Retention and erasure

Local data remains until it is changed or removed. To erase all local records, use Android Settings > Apps > CleanRoutine > Storage > Clear data. Android backup is disabled in this version. Clearing app data does not erase copies outside the app or network-provider service logs. In-app deletion and recurrence/undo controls are incomplete in this preview.

Adult testing and support

This testing release is intended for adults. If you contact support, your message and information you choose to include are handled to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed.
