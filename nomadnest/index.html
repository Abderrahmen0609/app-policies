NomadNest Privacy Policy

WeCareAboutYou · Updated 14 October 2026

Scope and publisher

NomadNest is published by WeCareAboutYou. This notice covers the device-local home/room workspace and optional hosted AI features in the 1.2.3 testing candidate. Contact contact@mellitinexus.com for privacy, deletion or output reports.

Local workspace

Room plans, imported photos/videos, captured frames and saved concepts are stored in a local Hive database. Local profile/favorites/searches/property drafts/inquiries/messages are a device-only demonstration, not a live marketplace. Document checklists store filenames/sizes rather than uploading document contents. The app adds no separate encryption layer to these local records. Platform backup/storage behavior may apply.

Optional authentication

AI requires a verified Melliti Nexus account through Supabase. Email-code/link verification or an existing password is processed over HTTPS. Session tokens remain in memory and are cleared at sign-out/restart. The service processes email, account identifiers and minimal authenticated request/abuse metadata. Passwords are not written into local room plans.

Explicit AI processing

Only when you request AI and agree to processing does a selected normalized room photo or captured still frame, with chosen room/furniture instructions, go through Supabase to OpenAI. Original image metadata is stripped during normalization. Original video is not uploaded for full-video generation. AI layouts/images are suggestions, not measured scans or guarantees. Importing media or signing in alone does not request an AI generation.

Providers and retention

The app service does not persist input images or completed AI images/layout results. Returned outputs are stored locally if you save them. Request metadata/digests have a seven-day window; daily cleanup can add up to a day, and separate short-lived counters prevent abuse/deletion from resetting allowance. Supabase Auth/platform logs and OpenAI processing can have separate retention practices; lack of app-server image storage is not a claim that all providers retain no data. The service uses authenticated HTTPS requests and the provider key stays on the server.

Output reporting

Report controls send the selected output/request reference when available, output kind, category and note you choose to a private support queue. Your room photo, clip, generated image, layout and AI brief are not automatically attached. A layout issue without a cloud request can be reported with no request reference. Reports are visible only to authorized service operators. Receipt does not guarantee a particular moderation action or response time. The service runs daily maintenance to remove reports after 90 days; scheduled runs may add up to a day.

Your deletion choices

Studio > Delete local room data removes local plans/media. AI > Delete AI service data clears app-specific request history; temporary abuse counters and separately submitted safety reports are handled as disclosed. The shared sign-in identity and other apps’ data remain. Request full account/associated-data deletion using the page below. The publisher verifies identity/scope and sends a completion notice; no fixed processing time is promised.

Sharing, catalog and audience

Export/sharing and retailer links hand selected content/requests to your chosen destination. Sample furniture prices and sponsored-placement labels are examples, with no paid partnership, commission or checkout. This adult planning/testing release does not make real bookings, purchases or payments, and is not intended for children. Measure furniture and room access yourself before acting on a proposal.
