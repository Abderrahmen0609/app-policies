BarberShop privacy policy

Barbershop Connect · Publisher: WeCareAboutYou · Operator: Melliti Nexus
Testing notice prepared 17 October 2026 · contact@mellitinexus.com

Which version you are using

The 1.5.3 offline guest preview uses bundled fictional people, bookings, products and articles. Changes in that preview are sample data in memory. It does not create an online account, connect to the app backend, upload photos, request location, contact other people, publish content or take payment. Reset or restart discards the sample changes. Do not enter real personal information in that preview.

The connected testing version adds real accounts and server-backed features described below. Feature availability depends on the testing service and your role. A sample preview, when offered, remains separately labelled. An app update does not turn fictional merchants, prices or appointments into real business offers.

Accounts and security

Connected sign-up and sign-in send your email address and password over HTTPS to Supabase Auth. The service keeps your account ID and confirmation/security state. Your profile includes your name, account type and optional biography or photo. The app does not save or display a plaintext copy of your password. Session credentials support authentication on your device. Supabase and authorized service operators process security information such as IP address, user agent and authentication events.

Email confirmation is required. Password recovery uses an emailed code entered in the app. An already-enrolled authentication factor may be required when deleting an account; a phone code is sent only if you request that factor. Do not send passwords, recovery codes or authentication tokens to support.

Listings, location and appointments

A barber can publish a business address, services, prices, facilities, working hours and a confirmed business map pin. These details are shown to other signed-in users. Device location is requested only after a barber chooses Use current location; device location/geocoding services help find the address, and only the confirmed business pin is stored. There is no background or continuous location tracking. You can enter the business information manually.

Appointment requests and history include the selected service, times, client/barber references and booking snapshots. Clients and their booked barbers can access their relevant appointments. Available times are calculated from schedules, breaks and blocks; clients do not receive a barber's private block reasons. Search text is sent with an authenticated request to find listings. The application database does not create a search-history record; infrastructure request logs have separate handling and retention.

Shops and private communications

Shop ownership and approved membership control shop access. Attendance, sales and expenses are role-limited operational records. An eligible new appointment can be stamped to the shop at booking; older appointments are not retrospectively exposed. A shop owner receives a limited customer view for shop-stamped bookings, rather than a customer's email, phone number, unrelated history or private barber-client chat.

Direct messages are visible to their conversation participants. Shop-customer messages are a separate customer/owner channel. Only an owner can post team announcements, and only current approved staff can access them. Removing a staff membership stops team-announcement access but does not erase an already-created ordinary owner-staff direct conversation, cancel an appointment or delete the person's app account.

Photos, reviews and community content

Selected profile and gallery photos first enter private storage for review. Only approved, server-re-encoded images receive public media URLs. Authorized reviewers can access pending submissions. Public photos can be copied by people who can see their URLs. Replacing an approved photo may leave an older copy while a legitimate appointment, review or report snapshot still references it.

Reviews, replies, likes and approved community posts are visible to other signed-in users. Pending/rejected community submissions are limited to their author and authorized reviewers. Hair or scalp information you voluntarily include may be health-related information. Do not include sensitive details or another person's information that you do not want reviewed or published. General hair-care material is not medical advice.

Reports, restricted content snapshots, blocks, moderation decisions and account restrictions support safety and abuse prevention. Report details are limited to the reporter and authorized operators; restricted evidence and internal case records have narrower operator access. A shop-message or announcement safety action reports the sender account, not a content-specific snapshot of that message. No continuous chat monitoring or automated detection is promised.

Orders, ledgers and external actions

Where enabled for testing, physical-product pickup requests record items, price snapshots, totals, status and buyer references. Shop sales/expenses are manually recorded business figures. The app does not collect card, bank-account or wallet credentials, execute a payment or verify that payment occurred. EUR is the current testing currency.

Directions open an external maps app or website only when requested. The system share sheet sends your selected public barber information, approved photo link or locally generated poster only to the destination you choose. Active public poster links expose the barber's display name, business address, rating, availability and optional review link to anyone holding the link; they do not expose the coordinate pair or private appointment records.

Providers and support

Supabase provides connected authentication, database, storage and related infrastructure. Public web pages and the testing web client are hosted using GitHub Pages; the hosting provider handles page requests. Email providers handle confirmation/recovery and support messages. External maps, browser, email and share destinations have their own practices. Information you choose to send to contact@mellitinexus.com is handled for support; uninstalling or deleting an app account does not automatically erase a support email.

This app source has no advertising SDK, advertising personalization, cross-app tracking SDK, contacts access, background-location service or payment-processing SDK. Android and Google Play separately handle installation, updates and platform diagnostics under their own settings.

Retention and deletion

Account and feature records remain while needed for their functions. You can edit relevant profile data, cancel pending photo submissions, remove your own supported content, and request account deletion. The deletion flow removes the authentication account and account-owned application records under the database relationships. Owned media is queued for cleanup across services; an outage or an existing permitted reference can delay removal. The app must not report immediate complete media erasure while cleanup is pending.

The current testing implementation does not automatically expire all safety cases, reported snapshots, decision/appeal history or suspension records. Stable identifiers and restricted safety evidence can remain after an account is deleted. Media-review audit/rate cleanup is separate from content-case retention. There is no fixed deletion-completion promise in this notice. Contact us about the scope of retained safety or support records and a deletion request. These testing limitations must be resolved into an approved and implemented lifecycle before unrestricted launch.

Request account deletion and read the steps. Do not assume that blocking someone, leaving a shop, clearing a device or uninstalling deletes your connected account or other participants' copies.

Your choices and audience

This testing service is intended for adults. Provide only the information needed for the test; use fictional business examples unless an actual participating business has agreed to provide its information. You can decline device location, choose which photos/content to submit, use report/block controls, contact support or request deletion. Contact contact@mellitinexus.com for privacy questions or a correction request.
