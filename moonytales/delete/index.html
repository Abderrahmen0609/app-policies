MoonyTales account and data deletion

Publisher: WeCareAboutYou · Updated 17 October 2026 · Google Play name: MonnyTales · Package: com.mellitinexus.dreamtales

Request deletion without reinstalling the app

Email contact@mellitinexus.com from the address associated with your MoonyTales account. Use the subject MoonyTales account deletion request. State whether you want the whole MoonyTales account removed or particular records corrected or removed. Do not send passwords, verification codes, child photos, medical documents or unnecessary family information.

The publisher must verify account ownership and the intended scope before removing records and confirm the outcome. If you no longer have access to the account email, explain that without sending identity documents unsolicited. This page opens an email request; it does not authenticate the sender, run deletion or promise a fixed completion time.

In the app

Open Settings → Delete Account and read the confirmation. The app requests deletion through its authenticated backend. Supported removal covers the account, owned profiles and family records, co-parent relationships, supported saved or generated content and account media. If the app reports an error, do not assume that all steps completed; contact support to verify the result.

Other copies and subscriptions

Device preferences, downloads, exported files, another recipient's copies, and provider backups or operational records are separate. Android Settings → Apps → MoonyTales/MonnyTales → Storage → Clear data removes local app storage, subject to platform behavior, but does not delete hosted records. Provider security logs, backups and transaction or legally required records can have separate retention.

Account deletion does not cancel Google Play or Apple subscriptions. Cancel through the store account used to purchase. A media link already issued can remain usable until expiry or removal of the underlying resource; downloaded copies cannot be recalled.
