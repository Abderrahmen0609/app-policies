FitBack Privacy Policy

Updated14 October2026. Applies to testing version1.0.2.

Scope

This policy covers the current FitBack testing version: gentle movement routines, optional on-device camera coaching, local exercise progress and a pain diary. Feedback is general movement guidance and is not a clinical assessment, diagnosis or medically validated form score. Reminders, premium upgrades and education-article links remain incomplete.

Local profile and health records

FitBack stores your chosen name, age range, work type, sitting hours, exercise-time preferences, exercise progress, pain-diary entries and session summaries in local preferences without additional app-level encryption. Pain entries can include symptoms/locations, stiffness, mood, sleep quality, sitting hours and notes. No cloud account or synchronization server is used.

Optional camera

If you start the camera coach and grant camera permission, live frames are processed on the device by Google ML Kit pose detection. FitBack does not record, save or upload photos, video or pose landmarks. It stores exercise repetition counts, timings and completion status. Audio is disabled in the app camera controller. Denying camera permission does not prevent you from browsing non-camera routines. Camera permission can be revoked in Android settings.

SDK diagnostics

Google ML Kit can send technical diagnostics, installation identifiers, device/app information, feature events and performance information to Google. Pose detection also uses Firebase Remote Config/Installations for configuration. This information supports SDK operation and diagnostics; it is separate from the local diary/profile and camera image content. SDK transmissions use HTTPS. FitBack contains no advertising SDK. Google explains its handling in the linked ML Kit and Firebase privacy documentation.

Retention and erasure

Local records remain until changed or removed. To erase local app data, use Android Settings > Apps > FitBack > Storage > Clear data. This version does not disable Android backup; device backup/transfer may retain copies and you should manage those copies through your device/backup provider. Erasing local data does not itself request deletion of Google SDK service logs or previously backed-up copies. The app does not offer a cloud account to delete. Contact support for data questions; do not send sensitive diary or camera content unless needed and specifically requested.

Adult testing and support

This testing release is intended for adults. If you contact support, your message and information you choose to include are handled to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed.
