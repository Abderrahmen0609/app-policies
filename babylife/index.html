BabyLife privacy policy

Publisher: WeCareAboutYou · Updated 16 October 2026

Version 1.1.0 is a testing update for local growth, feeding, diaper and sleep records. Earlier versions have fewer working controls. Cloud family sharing, export, configurable reminders and AI are unavailable. Use fictional information while testing.

Who operates this app

The Google Play publisher is WeCareAboutYou. Contact the publisher about privacy, support or your information at contact@mellitinexus.com.

Information on your device

BabyLife stores baby profiles (name, birth date, gender, optional weight, height and photo reference), feeding, diaper and sleep records, growth measurements, development progress and memories in this app’s local preferences. These records are not automatically sent to the developer or synchronized with another caregiver. This is not an encrypted medical-record system or family vault.

New installations of version 1.1.0 start empty. Example records retained from older versions are labelled as examples. You do not need an online account. The app does not add cloud family sharing, AI, billing or a notification service.

Network services and external links

The store-readiness release from version 1.0.2 bundles its Inter fonts and disables runtime font downloads. Earlier testing builds may fetch Inter font files from Google’s font hosting servers; those connections transmit request metadata such as the connection IP address. The app does not add baby, patient or care records to those requests.

Opening a privacy page or support email uses your browser or email application. Any information you choose to send in a support message is received by the publisher and your email provider. Avoid including unnecessary health or child-identifying information.

No advertising, functioning paid subscription or analytics integration is initialized in the reviewed app code. Platform services such as Google Play operate under their own policies.

Keeping and deleting local records

Records remain in local app storage until you remove them. Version 1.1.0 stores profiles and their current records together in one checked preferences document. Existing preference keys are retained as migration originals; editing or deleting a current record does not also erase those originals. If stored information cannot be read or a save cannot be confirmed, the app displays an error rather than silently replacing the workspace with examples.

Profile offers Clear local data with an explicit confirmation. It removes this app’s current preferences and retained migration originals, including profiles, tracking records and personal notes, and restarts the local workspace without reloading example people. If erasure is interrupted, the app blocks further record use until erasure is completed. It does not delete copies you saved outside the app or information you sent to support.

From version 1.1.0, deleting a profile also deletes its currently linked tracking, growth, memory and development records. Retained migration originals remain until Clear local data is completed. Older individual-profile deletion actions may leave linked entries. You can also use Android Settings → Apps → BabyLife → Storage → Clear data to erase the app workspace.

Backup and transfer

From the store-readiness release 1.0.2, Android app backup and preference transfer are excluded for this app. Earlier versions did not explicitly disable Android backup. Clearing current app data cannot erase any backup or device-transfer copies already created by Android or other tools; manage those through the relevant platform settings.

Health, adults and sensitive information

Version 1.1.0 is a local personal record keeper in testing. It supports separate child profiles, editable growth and tracking records, and daily sleep totals. These records are not clinical assessments or a substitute for professional care.

The app is for adult caregivers; children are not the intended operators. The app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions. It does not provide emergency response.

Support messages and retention

If you contact support, the publisher receives the information in your message to respond and investigate your request. Request deletion of those messages at the same address. Support correspondence remains in the publisher's mailbox until removed. You can request its removal at the contact address. Separately controlled platform/provider logs and backups follow those providers' privacy policies.
