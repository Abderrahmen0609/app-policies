CleanRoutine data and account deletion

Updated 16 October 2026. Household-sharing controls apply to version 1.1.0. Earlier 1.0.2 previews store records locally and have no CleanRoutine account or shared-household feature.

Delete only local device data

You can delete individual tasks or rooms in the app. To erase the complete local home, retained migration originals and saved device sign-in, open Android Settings > Apps > CleanRoutine > Storage > Clear data. Device erasure does not delete a shared household.

Delete CleanRoutine cloud data while keeping your sign-in account

Open CleanRoutine > Settings > Share a household, sign in, and choose Delete my CleanRoutine cloud data. Read and confirm the explanation. This deletes the households you own for all their members, removes your memberships and app invitation/rate-limit links, and anonymizes your completion identity in other owners' households. Your original device-local home stays on the device. The shared Auth account and data in other apps remain. Account-linked completion identifiers are anonymized. Room or task text you added, and local contributor names imported into someone else's household, can remain there; ask the household owner or support for content-specific removal if it identifies you. Records entered by other people and copies they kept may also remain.

Owners can instead select Delete shared household for one household. Members can choose Leave household to end access without deleting the owner's household records.

Request help or delete the shared Melliti Nexus account

Email contact@mellitinexus.com with subject “CleanRoutine deletion request”. Use the email associated with your sign-in and say whether you want only CleanRoutine data deleted while keeping your account, or deletion of the shared Melliti Nexus sign-in identity as well. You can make a request without installing the app.

We verify that you own the account without asking for your password or email code. Shared-identity deletion can affect sign-in to other connected Melliti Nexus apps; support explains that scope before processing it. Do not include task notes, invitation codes, passwords or unrelated personal documents. We confirm the request's outcome and explain any applicable retention exception.

What may remain

Other people's records and copies, support messages needed to handle your request, legally required records, and provider service/security logs may have separate retention. Invitation codes expire after 72 hours regardless of cleanup. A daily server cleanup is configured to remove invitation metadata seven days after expiry and join-attempt metadata older than 24 hours. Service interruptions can delay that cleanup; revoked access does not depend on it.
