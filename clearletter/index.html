ClearLetter Privacy Policy

Updated14 October2026. Applies to testing version1.0.2.

Scope

This policy covers the current ClearLetter closed-testing preview. It uses fixed sample letters, analysis examples and reply templates. It does not implement OCR, PDF extraction, content-based AI analysis, legal advice, cloud accounts or real subscription billing. Use disposable sample information only.

Information on your device

The app stores document title, sender, category, fixed sample text, demonstration analysis/deadline records, edited reply drafts, and local demo user/quota/subscription settings in app preferences without additional app-level encryption. Photo/PDF selection does not retain an original file in the document vault or read its contents. System camera/picker components may create temporary local/cache copies governed by Android and their respective applications.

Permissions and network services

The app bundles its fonts and sample assets. It does not download fonts at runtime and does not send local task or document records to an account, synchronization or AI server. This version contains no advertising or analytics SDK. Opening the online privacy policy or an email app is a user-initiated external action; the destination service handles connection or message information under its own policies.

Retention and erasure

Local data remains until changed or removed. The current Delete All Documents control is incomplete and should not be used as proof that stored records were erased. To erase all local records, use Android Settings > Apps > ClearLetter > Storage > Clear data. Android backup is disabled. Clearing app data does not remove copies outside this app, system picker/camera copies, or network-provider service logs.

Adult testing and support

This testing release is intended for adults. If you contact support, your message and information you choose to include are handled to respond to your request. Avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed.
